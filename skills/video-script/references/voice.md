# Andrew's on-camera voice

Built from his real takes (roofer-leads recordings, 2025-08-05). Grow it: after every
recording, paste 5–10 of his best natural lines into **Real lines** below.

## How he actually talks
- **Starts lines with connectors:** "So…", "Alright…", "Now…", "And…", "But…". Lines
  link to each other; they aren't standalone slogans.
- **Explains the why, in plain cause and effect:** "Because we've made zero dollars so
  far, we're using a free tool." "As soon as we make our first sale, we're gonna upgrade."
- **"We", not "you should":** brings the viewer along, never lectures.
- **Casual, slightly loose grammar:** "gonna", "real quick", "a lot more", "or so",
  "like", sentences that run on a bit. He hedges honestly: "probably", "about fifty
  percent", "it looks like we got…".
- **Concrete over clever:** tool names, prices, counts ("226", "eleven hundred roofers",
  "one city"). Numbers do the work, not adjectives.
- **Dry, small humor:** "we had to bring the fancy camera out for this." Not punchlines.
- **Plain words:** "lousy", "completely free", "my personal favorite for price".
- **Talks to one person, out loud:** you could imagine him saying it to a friend in the car.

## What does NOT sound like him (AI tells in scripts)
`scripts/voice_check.py` flags these. Fix every one unless Andrew actually says it that way.
- **Slogan triplets:** "My body, my head, and my bank account. All three." "Post daily.
  Train. Outreach." Real people list things inside a normal sentence, if at all.
- **Mic-drop fragments:** "That's it." "All three." "Every single day." as their own lines.
- **Balanced/mirrored sentences:** "If it works, you'll see it. If it doesn't, you'll see
  that too." "Fired up on Monday, gone by Friday."
- **"Not X, it's Y" / "I'm not doing X. I'm doing Y."** framing.
- **Hype/self-help vocabulary:** unlock, leverage, game-changer, 10x, dive in, journey,
  transform, elevate, level up, mindset shift, "stupid simple", "here's the thing", "let
  that sink in", "the truth is", "real talk".
- **Em dashes and semicolons:** nobody speaks punctuation. Use "and", "but", "so".
- **Every line the same length:** real speech mixes a long rambly line with a short one.
- **No connectors:** five lines in a row that each start cold read like captions, not talk.
- **Borrowed phrasing from the reference video** — structure only, never their wording.
